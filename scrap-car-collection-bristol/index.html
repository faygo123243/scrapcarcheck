How quickly can I get my car collected in Bristol?

Most licensed ATFs covering Bristol can collect within 24 to 48 hours of you accepting a quote. Same day collection is sometimes available if you get your quote in the morning. The dealer will contact you directly to agree a time that works for both parties.

Do Bristol scrap dealers cover all local postcodes (BS, BA)?

Yes. The dealers on our platform cover all BS postcodes across the city and surrounding suburbs, as well as BA postcodes covering Bath and the wider Somerset area. If you are in a rural location on the outskirts, collection is still free but you should confirm the area with the dealer when they contact you.

Are there many licensed ATFs near Bristol?

Bristol has a good concentration of Environment Agency licensed ATFs. The region's vehicle density supports a competitive market with multiple operators, which tends to keep prices strong. All dealers on Scrap Car Check are verified ATFs, so you never have to worry about using an unlicensed collector and remaining liable for the vehicle.

What is the average scrap car price in Bristol right now?

For the most common vehicles on Bristol roads, such as a family hatchback or supermini, you can typically expect £225 to £360 at current steel rates. Heavier vehicles including large SUVs and vans achieve higher prices.
