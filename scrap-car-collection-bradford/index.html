How to scrap your car in Bradford

Enter your registration plate and postcode, compare the quotes that come back, and confirm the one you want. A flatbed truck will come to your address at the agreed time. The driver inspects the car, takes the keys and issues your Certificate of Destruction. Payment arrives by bank transfer the same day or the next working day.

Non-runners and cars without an MOT are collected in exactly the same way. See: How to scrap a car that does not run.

Getting the most for your car in Bradford

Comparing multiple dealers is the single biggest thing you can do to improve your return. In a city like Bradford with several active dealers, quotes for the same car regularly differ by £30 to £60. Leave the catalytic converter in place and make sure the battery is connected if possible — both add value to the offer. For more on pricing, see: How much is my scrap car worth?
