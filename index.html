Common Questions

Scrap car prices are based primarily on the weight of your vehicle multiplied by the current scrap steel and non-ferrous metal price. Your car's make, model and condition also affect the final figure — some models retain parts value that adds to the scrap metal price.

Yes. All dealers in our network offer free collection within their operating area. If you are outside a dealer's zone, they may charge a small collection fee, which will always be disclosed before you accept a quote.

You do not need your V5C to scrap your car — a licensed ATF can still take it. However, you should tell the DVLA that you have scrapped the vehicle. Your scrapping dealer will usually give you a Certificate of Destruction (CoD) which you can use to notify the DVLA and claim any remaining tax.

Most authorised scrap dealers pay by bank transfer on the day of collection or the next working day. Cash in hand is no longer legal for scrap metal sales in England, Scotland and Wales.

Yes. All the dealers on our platform collect non-runners. They will use a flatbed truck or trailer. Just select "non-runner" or "accident damaged" when you fill in your condition, and your quotes will reflect this.

All dealers on Scrap Car Check are registered Authorised Treatment Facilities (ATFs). They legally must depollute the car — draining all fluids safely — before crushing or shredding it. Recyclable parts are recovered and sold; the metal is smelted and reused. End-of-life vehicles in the UK must be processed this way under the ELV Regulations 2003.
