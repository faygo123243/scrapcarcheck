How to scrap your car in Wolverhampton

Enter your registration number into our form and you will instantly see quotes from licensed dealers collecting in your area. Pick the offer you want, confirm a collection time, and the driver will bring a Certificate of Destruction and arrange payment by bank transfer. Once the car has gone, notify the DVLA using the V5C reference number. For a full guide to the process, see how to scrap a car. If your car does not run, see our guide to scrapping a non runner.

Getting the best scrap price in Wolverhampton

The Black Country has a long tradition of metal processing, and active competition between dealers here means quotes for the same car regularly differ by £30 to £60. Our form shows which dealers are quoting in your postcode at that moment, so you can quickly spot the best offer without making multiple phone calls. Leave the catalytic converter and battery in place before collection, as stripping either reduces the quoted price.
