How to scrap your car in Plymouth

The process is straightforward. Enter your registration number into our form and you will receive quotes from licensed dealers who are actively collecting in your area. After collection in Plymouth

Once your car has been collected, you must tell the DVLA you no longer own it. This is done online using the V5C logbook reference number and takes about two minutes. If you do not notify the DVLA, you remain liable for any road tax or penalties. The dealer will have issued your Certificate of Destruction, which is your proof that the vehicle was scrapped legally. Keep this document safe. For full DVLA notification steps, see our guide to DVLA notification after scrapping.
