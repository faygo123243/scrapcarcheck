How to Get the Best Scrap Price

A few simple steps can make a meaningful difference to what you receive. Here are the four things that matter most.

1

Get at least three quotes

The gap between the lowest and highest quote for the same car can be £150 or more. Always compare before accepting, even if the first offer sounds reasonable.

2

Check scrap metal prices first

Scrap values are tied to live steel and alloy prices. Checking the spot price before you sell means you know whether the market is in your favour right now.

3

Remove valuable parts in advance

Items like a spare tyre, sat nav, or aftermarket stereo can be sold separately and are worth more than the scrap yard will pay for them. Take them out beforehand.

4

Only use an authorised ATF

Scrapping with an unlicensed buyer is illegal and leaves you liable for the vehicle. An authorised ATF gives you a Certificate of Destruction and notifies the DVLA.
