Are there many licensed ATFs near Birmingham?

The West Midlands has one of the highest concentrations of Environment Agency licensed ATFs in England. This is partly a legacy of the region's industrial and automotive manufacturing history. Greater competition between yards benefits sellers, as dealers typically offer stronger prices to secure vehicles over their rivals.
