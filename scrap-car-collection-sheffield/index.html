Why Use Scrap Car Check in Sheffield?

  • Competing dealer quotes: Sheffield and South Yorkshire have a high density of licensed ATFs. We put them in competition with each other so you get the best available price rather than the first offer you come across.
  • Steel processing proximity: Sheffield's proximity to major scrap steel and metal processing facilities means dealers can often pay a premium compared to more rural areas, because their haulage costs to the processor are lower.
  • Licensed ATFs only: Every dealer on our platform holds a valid Environment Agency licence. You receive a Certificate of Destruction, the DVLA is notified, and any remaining road tax is automatically refunded to you.
  • Same day bank transfer: Under the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013, all payments must be made by bank transfer or cheque. Our dealers pay promptly, usually on the day of collection, and never in cash.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get my car collected in Sheffield?

Most licensed dealers covering Sheffield can collect within 24 hours of accepting a quote, and many offer same day collection if you get your quote in early. You choose a collection slot that works for you when the dealer contacts you to book in.

Do Sheffield scrap dealers cover all South Yorkshire postcodes (S, DN, S60 to S66)?

Yes. Our dealer network covers the full range of S and DN postcodes, including the Rotherham zones S60 to S66 and the Doncaster DN1 to DN12 areas. If you are in a more rural part of South Yorkshire such as Penistone or the Upper Don Valley, collection is still available though it may be a next day service rather than same day.

Does Sheffield's steel heritage affect scrap car prices?

It can work in your favour. Sheffield and the wider South Yorkshire corridor has a high concentration of metal recycling and steel processing businesses. This means local ATFs have shorter transport distances to get your scrap to a processor, which can result in slightly stronger prices compared to areas where dealers have to haul scrap further. That said, the biggest driver of your individual price is the weight and condition of your specific vehicle and the current steel and aluminium spot price.

What is the average scrap car price in Sheffield right now?

For a typical medium sized car in the S or DN postcode area, prices currently sit in the range of £200 to £350 depending on weight and condition. Larger vehicles and SUVs can attract £350 to £520.
