ULEZ and Older Vehicles

London's Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) has driven a significant increase in cars being scrapped early. Vehicles that fail the ULEZ emissions standard may not be worth repairing for London use, and scrapping can be the most practical option. The scrap value is based on the steel weight and condition of the vehicle, not the emissions standard — a non ULEZ compliant car can still fetch a good price at a licensed ATF.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get my car collected in London?
Most licensed ATFs covering London can collect within 24 to 48 hours. For inner London boroughs, same day collection is often available if you book before midday. Collection times vary depending on your specific borough and the dealer's schedule, but our comparison tool shows estimated collection windows alongside each quote.
Do London scrap dealers collect from all boroughs?
Yes. Our dealer network covers all 32 London boroughs including inner London areas such as Hackney, Islington and Southwark as well as outer boroughs such as Havering, Hillingdon and Bromley. Entering your postcode when you request a quote ensures you are only matched with dealers who can realistically collect from your location.
Does ULEZ affect my scrap car price?
No. Scrap car prices are calculated on the weight of recoverable steel and the general condition of the vehicle, not on its emissions rating or ULEZ compliance. A pre 2006 diesel that would cost you the daily ULEZ charge to drive in London still has the same scrap value as any other vehicle of similar weight. Scrapping is often the most financially sensible exit route for a non compliant vehicle if repair costs are not justified.
What is the average scrap car price in London right now?
London scrap prices track the national average closely. For a typical family hatchback in reasonable condition, you should expect somewhere between £225 and £360 at current steel prices. A large SUV or 4x4 can fetch £390 to £620. Prices shift with global steel demand, so the best way to get an accurate figure is to run a live quote through the tool above, which queries multiple dealers in real time.
