How to scrap your car in Newcastle

Enter your registration plate and postcode on our comparison form. You will see quotes from dealers covering the Newcastle area in seconds. Select the best offer, confirm the collection details, and a flatbed truck will come to your address. The driver inspects the car, confirms the price, takes the keys, and hands you your Certificate of Destruction. Payment follows by bank transfer, usually on the day.

Non-running cars, accident-damaged vehicles and cars with no MOT are all collected without any issue. The dealer uses a flatbed in every case. See our full guide: How to scrap a car: step by step.

Getting the best scrap price in Newcastle

The North East has a healthy concentration of licensed ATFs, particularly around the Team Valley and along the River Tyne. Competition between dealers is genuine, and quotes for the same car can differ by £30 to £60. A dealer whose truck is already in your area will often offer more than one making a dedicated trip. Our form shows you which dealers are actively quoting in your postcode at that moment, which is the easiest way to find out who is offering the most.

Leave the catalytic converter in place before collection. Removing it does not increase your return and will typically reduce the quote from any dealer who notices it is missing. For a full explanation of how pricing works, see our guide: How much is my scrap car worth?

After your car is collected in Newcastle

Once the ATF has collected and issued your Certificate of Destruction, notify the DVLA online at gov.uk. This cancels the vehicle in your name and triggers an automatic refund of any remaining road tax. The process takes around two minutes.
