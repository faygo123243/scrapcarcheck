  • Multiple competing dealers covering every Manchester postcode means you see the strongest available price rather than accepting the first offer you are given.
  • Every dealer on our platform is a licensed ATF registered with the Environment Agency. You get a Certificate of Destruction, which proves the car has been legally scrapped and removes your liability as keeper.

Manchester Scrap Car FAQs

How quickly can I get my car collected in Manchester?

Most Manchester ATFs can collect within 24 to 48 hours of you accepting a quote. Same day collection is often available if you confirm your booking before midday. The city's density of dealers means there is almost always one nearby with availability at short notice.

Do Manchester scrap dealers collect from all postcodes including M, SK, WN, BL and OL?

Yes. The ATFs on our platform cover all Manchester postcodes including M (Manchester city centre and inner suburbs), SK (Stockport), WN (Wigan), BL (Bolton) and OL (Oldham and Rochdale). If you are on the edge of Greater Manchester or in a neighbouring area such as Warrington or Macclesfield, we can still match you with a dealer willing to collect.

Are there many licensed ATFs near Manchester?

Yes. Greater Manchester has one of the highest concentrations of Environment Agency registered ATFs outside London. This is partly a legacy of the region's industrial history and the volume of vehicles in circulation across the conurbation. More licensed buyers competing for your car is good for you because it keeps prices competitive and collection times short.

What is the average scrap car price in Manchester right now?

Based on current steel rates of around £145 per tonne, a typical family hatchback in Manchester fetches between £225 and £360. A larger SUV or 4x4 can reach £480 to £620. Prices shift with the steel market, so the best way to see your current price is to enter your reg plate above and get live quotes from local dealers rather than relying on a static estimate.
