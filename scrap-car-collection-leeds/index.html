Leeds scrap car FAQ

How quickly can I get my car collected in Leeds?

Most licensed ATFs serving the Leeds area can collect within 24 to 48 hours of you accepting a quote. Some dealers offer same day collection if you book before midday. Collection times can vary during busy periods so it is worth asking the dealer when you confirm the booking.

Do Leeds scrap dealers cover all West Yorkshire postcodes (LS, BD, WF, HD, HX)?

Yes. The dealers on our platform collectively cover all West Yorkshire postcode areas. Individual dealers may have a preferred radius but by comparing multiple offers you will find one that covers your exact location. Enter your postcode when you get a quote and we only show you dealers who can reach you.

Are there many licensed ATFs near Leeds?

West Yorkshire has a well established network of Authorised Treatment Facilities, reflecting its industrial heritage and vehicle density. Leeds, Bradford and Wakefield all have multiple Environment Agency registered ATFs operating in and around them. Using a licensed ATF is a legal requirement: only they can issue the Certificate of Destruction that formally removes your liability for the vehicle.

What is the average scrap car price in Leeds right now?

The average for a typical family hatchback in the Leeds area is currently around £270 to £320, based on September 2026 steel market rates. Larger vehicles such as SUVs and vans will return more. Prices move with the global steel market so the best way to get an accurate figure is to run a quick quote with your registration number.
