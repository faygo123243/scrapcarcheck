How to scrap your car in Nottingham

Enter your registration plate and postcode on our comparison form. You will see quotes from dealers covering the Nottingham area within seconds. Select the best offer, confirm the collection details, and a flatbed truck will come to your address. The driver inspects the car, confirms the price, takes the keys, and hands you your Certificate of Destruction. Payment follows by bank transfer, usually on the day.

Whether your car runs or not makes no difference to how we collect. Non-runners, cars with failed MOTs, and accident-damaged vehicles are all accepted. See our guide: How to scrap a car that does not run.

Why compare dealers in Nottingham?

The East Midlands has a strong concentration of licensed ATFs, which means genuine competition for your vehicle. Will I get less if I am further from Nottingham?

Possibly, though not always by much. Dealers further from your location have higher collection costs, which can reduce their offer slightly. In practice, if your car is in an area covered by our network, dealers will account for collection costs in their quoted price — there are no hidden charges added later. The price you accept is the price you receive.
