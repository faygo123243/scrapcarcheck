Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

Scrap Car Check ("we", "our", "us") operates scrapcarcheck.co.uk. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information.

What information we collect

When you use our quote service, we collect the following information:

  • Your vehicle registration number (used to identify your vehicle)
  • Your postcode (used to match you with local dealers)
  • Vehicle condition (used to generate accurate quotes)
  • Your name and mobile number (shared with dealers so they can contact you directly)

How we use your information

We share your registration, postcode, vehicle condition, name and phone number with up to three licensed ATF dealers in our network so they can prepare a quote and contact you directly. We do not sell your data to third parties outside our dealer network.

Cookies

We use only essential session cookies required for the site to function. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete any personal data we hold. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@scrapcarcheck.co.uk.

Contact

For privacy questions: privacy@scrapcarcheck.co.uk
