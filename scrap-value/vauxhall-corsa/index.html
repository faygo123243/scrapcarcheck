How Much Is a Vauxhall Corsa Worth as Scrap in 2026?

Scrap values for the Vauxhall Corsa, what drives the price, and how to get the best offer from a licensed ATF.

A Vauxhall Corsa in scrap condition is typically worth between £80 and £200 in 2026. As one of the UK's most popular superminis, the Corsa is a familiar car in any scrap yard, which means straightforward pricing and quick collection. Its lighter kerbweight compared to a medium hatchback means the scrap value sits in the lower range, but for a small car it holds up well thanks to a solid steel construction and a usable catalytic converter on petrol versions.

Vauxhall Corsa scrap value in 2026: Local scrap prices for a Vauxhall Corsa typically range from £80 to £200 depending on condition, mileage and current scrap metal prices.

What Affects the Scrap Value of a Vauxhall Corsa?

A standard Vauxhall Corsa weighs between 1,060 and 1,250 kg, depending on engine and generation. Older Corsa D and Corsa C models are among the lightest, while the larger Corsa E and more recent Corsa F come in heavier. The steel content is the primary basis for the scrap offer, so heavier configurations will naturally attract a higher price per tonne of scrap steel.

The catalytic converter on a petrol Corsa adds a meaningful premium above the base metal price. A working cat in reasonable condition can contribute £20 to £60 to the overall offer, and leaving it in place ensures that value is included in the quote you receive. Removing the cat yourself before sale almost always results in a lower total payment.

Non-runners are collected without any issue and fetch nearly the same scrap price as a car that drives. The V5C logbook is worth keeping handy as it simplifies the handover, but its absence does not prevent collection. High mileage has almost no effect on scrap value, as a scrap dealer is buying the steel and precious metals rather than the mechanical condition of the engine.

Catalytic Converter Value

The Vauxhall Corsa uses a single catalytic converter. On older pre-Euro 5 models the cat tends to have a higher precious metal content and can be worth £25 to £55 to a refiner. More recent Corsa models carry a smaller, more efficient cat worth roughly £15 to £40. Diesel Corsas use a diesel oxidation catalyst with a lower precious metal yield. Whichever variant you have, the cat should always stay on the car when you hand it over for scrap.

How to Get the Best Price for Your Vauxhall Corsa

Comparing three or more quotes is the most effective step you can take. Even on a smaller car like the Corsa, the spread between offers can be £20 to £40, which represents a significant percentage of the total. Use a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility to ensure payment by bank transfer and to receive a Certificate of Destruction, and keep the V5C logbook to hand on collection day. The cat should stay in place and the battery should ideally be connected to help the dealer assess the car accurately.
