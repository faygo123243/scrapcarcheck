How Much Is a Vauxhall Astra Worth as Scrap in 2026?

Scrap values for the Vauxhall Astra, what drives the price, and how to get the best offer from a licensed ATF.

A Vauxhall Astra in scrap condition is typically worth between £120 and £280 in 2026. The Astra is one of the most scrapped cars in the UK, which means dealers are familiar with its weight and parts profile and can price it quickly and accurately. As a medium hatchback with a solid steel bodyshell, it returns a reliable mid-range scrap price that compares well to similar European rivals.

Vauxhall Astra scrap value in 2026: Local scrap prices for a Vauxhall Astra typically range from £120 to £280 depending on condition, mileage and current scrap metal prices.

What Affects the Scrap Value of a Vauxhall Astra?

The steel content of the car is what a scrap dealer primarily pays for. A standard Vauxhall Astra hatchback weighs between 1,280 and 1,500 kg depending on model year and specification, giving it a reasonable tonnage for a family hatchback. Diesel variants and estates sit at the heavier end of that range and usually attract slightly higher offers as a result.

The catalytic converter is the second most important factor for petrol Astras. Scrap dealers either process the cat themselves or sell it on to a specialist, and the precious metal content adds a measurable amount to the overall offer. A cat in good working order on an older Astra can contribute £30 to £70 to the final price, so it is always worth leaving it in place.

The running condition of the car and the presence of the V5C logbook both have a small but real effect. Non-runners are collected without issue, but the steel price at any given moment is a bigger variable than whether the engine starts. Heavy surface corrosion can reduce the useful metal content slightly, but it rarely changes the price dramatically for a sound structural shell.

Catalytic Converter Value

The Vauxhall Astra carries a single catalytic converter. Older models from the early 2000s tend to have a higher precious metal loading, which makes their cats more valuable to specialist refiners. More recent Astras use smaller, lighter cats with a lower metal yield. As a rough guide, expect a cat from a pre-2010 Astra to be worth £40 to £70, and one from a post-2015 model to sit closer to £20 to £50. In either case, leave the cat on the car when you hand it over.

How to Get the Best Price for Your Vauxhall Astra

Comparing multiple offers is the most reliable way to improve your return. Because the Astra is such a common car, dealers know exactly what they are getting and price accordingly, so the difference between the lowest and highest offers tends to narrow compared to rarer models. Even so, comparing three or four quotes can still reveal a gap of £20 to £50. Use a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility, have the V5C to hand, and leave the catalytic converter in place to make the transaction as smooth and well-paid as possible.
