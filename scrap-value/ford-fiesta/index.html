How Much Is a Ford Fiesta Worth as Scrap in 2026?

Scrap values for the Ford Fiesta, what drives the price, and how to get the best offer from a licensed ATF.

A Ford Fiesta in scrap condition is typically worth between £80 and £200 in 2026. The Fiesta is one of the most scrapped cars in the UK, and as a small hatchback it falls in the lighter weight category, which places its scrap value below that of larger family cars. That said, the Fiesta's widespread popularity means dealers are comfortable pricing it quickly and collection is generally straightforward.

Ford Fiesta scrap value in 2026: Local scrap prices for a Ford Fiesta typically range from £80 to £200 depending on condition, mileage and current scrap metal prices.

What Affects the Scrap Value of a Ford Fiesta?

The Fiesta weighs between 1,050 and 1,250 kg depending on engine size, body style and model year. That lighter kerbweight compared to a medium hatchback means there is less steel per car, which is the main reason its scrap value sits lower than a Focus or Astra. Three door variants tend to be slightly lighter than five door versions, and the standard petrol 1.0 litre EcoBoost is among the lightest configurations available.

The catalytic converter still plays a meaningful role despite the car's smaller size. A working cat in good condition can add £20 to £60 to the overall offer, so leaving it on the car before collection is always the right call. Older Fiestas with a pre-Euro 5 cat tend to carry a slightly higher precious metal loading than more modern models.

The car does not need to be a runner to be collected or to fetch close to its full scrap value. Non-runners are picked up by flatbed in the same way as driveable cars, and the scrap price is based on the metal content rather than whether the engine works. Age and visible corrosion have a small downward effect, but a sound underbody will fetch nearly the same price regardless of mileage.

Catalytic Converter Value

The Ford Fiesta uses a single catalytic converter fitted close to the exhaust manifold. On older models from the early to mid 2000s, the cat can be worth £30 to £60 to a specialist refiner. Post-2012 Fiestas carry a smaller, more modern cat that typically yields £15 to £40. As with all petrol cars, the cat should always be left in place when you hand the car over for scrap to make sure its full value is included in the offer you receive.

How to Get the Best Price for Your Ford Fiesta

Comparing at least three quotes is particularly worthwhile for a Fiesta because, while the car is common, the spread between the lowest and highest offers can still be £20 to £40 on a small hatchback. Use a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility rather than an unlicensed collector to ensure your payment comes by bank transfer and that a Certificate of Destruction is properly issued. Keep the V5C logbook with the car and leave the catalytic converter in place on collection day.
