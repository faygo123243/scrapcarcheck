How Much Is a Volkswagen Polo Worth as Scrap in 2026?

Scrap values for the Volkswagen Polo, what drives the price, and how to get the best offer from a licensed ATF.

A Volkswagen Polo in scrap condition is typically worth between £80 and £200 in 2026. The Polo sits in the supermini class, which means a lighter kerbweight and a lower scrap value than the larger Golf, but its VW Group build quality gives it a solid steel construction that holds up well through the scrapping process. Dealers who regularly handle VW Group cars can value it quickly, and the cat on petrol variants contributes a useful premium above the base steel price.

Volkswagen Polo scrap value in 2026: Local scrap prices for a Volkswagen Polo typically range from £80 to £200 depending on condition, mileage and current scrap metal prices.

What Affects the Scrap Value of a Volkswagen Polo?

The Polo weighs between 1,000 and 1,280 kg across its generations. Older Polo models such as the Mk4 are at the lighter end, while the larger Mk6 generation introduced in 2017 is notably heavier and returns a correspondingly better scrap price. Any Polo with a diesel engine will tend to be slightly heavier than the equivalent petrol model, which gives it a small advantage in terms of steel content.

The catalytic converter is a meaningful contributor to the total offer on petrol Polos. The VW Group cats are well regarded by refiners for their precious metal loading, and a working cat in good condition on an older Polo can add £20 to £60 to the price. Leaving the cat in place before collection is always the right approach, as the dealer factors its value into the quoted price and any removal before the handover reduces what you receive.

Non-runners are collected without issue, and the scrap value of a Polo is not significantly reduced by whether it starts. High mileage has almost no effect on the scrap offer because the steel and precious metal content is what matters. The V5C logbook is useful to have on the day and can speed up the process, though its absence does not prevent the collection from going ahead.

Catalytic Converter Value

The Volkswagen Polo uses a single catalytic converter positioned close to the engine. On older Mk4 and Mk5 models the cat carries a higher precious metal loading and can be worth £25 to £55. More recent Mk6 Polos carry a modern efficient cat worth roughly £15 to £40. VW Group cats are generally well received by refiners, so keeping the cat in place gives the dealer a clear and full asset to price into the offer.

How to Get the Best Price for Your Volkswagen Polo

Even for a lighter small hatchback, comparing multiple quotes is worthwhile. The spread between the lowest and highest offers for a Polo can be £20 to £40, which is a meaningful percentage of the total value. Use a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility to ensure your payment arrives by bank transfer and that a Certificate of Destruction is properly issued. Keep the V5C logbook handy and leave the catalytic converter in place before the collection arrives.
