How Much Is a Toyota Prius Worth as Scrap in 2026?

Scrap values for the Toyota Prius, why hybrids fetch more than standard petrol cars, and how to get the best offer from a licensed ATF.

A Toyota Prius in scrap condition is typically worth between £150 and £400 in 2026. The Prius sits noticeably higher than comparable petrol-only cars in the same weight class for two main reasons: its hybrid battery pack contains recoverable materials with real commodity value, and it carries a high specification catalytic converter setup that attracts strong interest from precious metal refiners. If the hybrid battery is in good condition and the cats are intact, a Prius can fetch close to the upper end of that range.

Toyota Prius scrap value in 2026: Local scrap prices for a Toyota Prius typically range from £150 to £400 depending on condition, mileage, hybrid battery health and current scrap metal prices.

What Affects the Scrap Value of a Toyota Prius?

The Prius weighs between 1,350 and 1,500 kg across its generations, which puts it in the same ballpark as a medium hatchback for steel content. What separates it from a standard petrol car at the point of scrapping is the hybrid battery. The nickel metal hydride batteries used in older Prius models and the lithium-ion units in the Prius from the fourth generation onwards both contain recoverable metals that add a premium above the base steel price. A working battery in good condition is worth more to a dealer than a degraded or dead one, so it is worth mentioning the battery state when you request quotes.

The catalytic converter setup on the Prius is more complex than a standard petrol car and contributes meaningfully to the overall offer. Keeping both cats intact and in place before collection is essential if you want to receive the full premium that a Prius commands over a comparable conventionally powered car.

Running condition matters somewhat more for a Prius than for a purely steel-valued car, because a dealer assessing the hybrid battery and catalyst condition will want to know the car's history. Non-runners are still collected and still fetch a solid price, but expect some variation in offers depending on how the dealer evaluates the battery condition without being able to run the car up.

Catalytic Converter Value

The Toyota Prius is notable for carrying two catalytic converters: a main catalyst close to the engine and a second underfloor unit. Together these two cats contain a meaningful loading of platinum, palladium and rhodium, making the Prius one of the better petrol hybrid cars for cat value. Depending on the generation and condition, the combined cat value on a Prius can reach £80 to £150, which is substantially higher than a comparable conventional hatchback. Both cats must stay on the car when you hand it over to ensure the full precious metal value is reflected in the offer.

How to Get the Best Price for Your Toyota Prius

Because the Prius has components that require specialist knowledge to value accurately, it is particularly important to compare quotes from multiple licensed Authorised Treatment Facilities rather than settling for the first offer. A dealer with experience handling hybrid vehicles will factor in the battery and the twin cats properly; one that does not may undervalue the car. Get at least three quotes, mention the battery condition if you know it, and ensure all catalysts and the battery pack remain in place on collection day. Have the V5C logbook ready to smooth the handover.
