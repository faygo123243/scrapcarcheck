How Much Is a Ford Focus Worth as Scrap in 2026?

Scrap values for the Ford Focus, what drives the price, and how to get the best offer from a licensed ATF.

A Ford Focus in scrap condition is typically worth between £120 and £280 in 2026. The Focus sits in the medium hatchback class, which gives it a reasonable steel content and a usable catalytic converter, so it tends to return a solid mid-range scrap price. The exact figure depends on the weight of your specific model, the condition of the cat, and where scrap metal prices sit on the day you sell.

Ford Focus scrap value in 2026: Local scrap prices for a Ford Focus typically range from £120 to £280 depending on condition, mileage and current scrap metal prices.

What Affects the Scrap Value of a Ford Focus?

Weight is the primary driver of scrap value, because most of what a scrapper pays for is the steel in the bodyshell and mechanical components. A Ford Focus weighs around 1,300 to 1,450 kg depending on trim and body style, which puts it comfortably in the middle of the hatchback market. Heavier diesel estate variants will generally fetch slightly more than a lighter petrol hatchback.

The condition of your catalytic converter matters a great deal. The cat contains small amounts of precious metals such as platinum, palladium and rhodium, and a good working cat can add £20 to £80 to the offer on its own. If the cat has been removed before you sell, most dealers will reduce their offer to reflect that loss.

Whether the car runs also has a bearing, though less so than people expect. A non-runner will still fetch most of its scrap value because the steel content is the same regardless. Age and mileage matter insofar as a heavily corroded body reduces the usable steel, but they do not affect price as strongly as they would in a part-exchange situation.

Catalytic Converter Value

The Ford Focus uses a single catalytic converter fitted close to the engine. On older pre-2010 models the cat tends to contain more precious metal loading and can be worth £40 to £80 in isolation. Post-2015 models carry a smaller, more modern cat that typically fetches £20 to £50. Always keep the cat on the car when you sell it for scrap: removing it yourself rarely produces a better overall return and can complicate the legal handover.

How to Get the Best Price for Your Ford Focus

The most effective step is to compare at least three quotes before you commit. Scrap prices vary between dealers even in the same postcode area, and a five minute comparison regularly uncovers a difference of £30 to £50. Use a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility rather than an unlicensed collector, as ATFs are legally required to pay by bank transfer and to issue a Certificate of Destruction. Keep the V5C logbook with the car and leave the catalytic converter in place, both of which help maximise the offer and speed up the handover on collection day.
