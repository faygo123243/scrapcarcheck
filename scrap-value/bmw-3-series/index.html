How Much Is a BMW 3 Series Worth as Scrap in 2026?

Scrap values for the BMW 3 Series, what drives the price, and how to get the best offer from a licensed ATF.

A BMW 3 Series in scrap condition is typically worth between £150 and £350 in 2026. As an executive saloon, the 3 Series sits in a heavier weight class than most family hatchbacks, and its larger steel content and higher spec catalytic converters both push the scrap value above what you would expect from a standard compact car. Touring estate variants, which are heavier still, tend to attract offers towards the upper end of that range.

BMW 3 Series scrap value in 2026: Local scrap prices for a BMW 3 Series typically range from £150 to £350 depending on condition, mileage and current scrap metal prices.

What Affects the Scrap Value of a BMW 3 Series?

The 3 Series weighs between 1,400 and 1,700 kg depending on body style, generation and drivetrain. Diesel models and Touring estates sit at the heavier end, while lighter petrol saloons and coupes come in a little lower. That extra weight translates directly into more steel, which is the primary commodity a scrap dealer pays for when they take your car away.

The catalytic converter on a BMW 3 Series is a meaningful contributor to the offer. BMW fits high quality cats to comply with strict European emissions standards, and the precious metal loading on older models in particular can be substantial. A working cat in good condition on a pre-2015 3 Series can add £50 to £120 to the overall price, making it one of the most valuable individual components on the car. More recent models carry smaller, more efficient cats worth somewhat less, but still well worth keeping in place.

Running condition matters less than many owners expect. A non-starting 3 Series is still worth nearly as much as a runner because the scrap value is driven by the steel and precious metal content, not whether the engine fires up. The V5C logbook is worth having ready, and excessive corrosion to the underbody can reduce the usable steel slightly, but neither factor changes the price dramatically for a structurally sound car.

Catalytic Converter Value

The BMW 3 Series uses a single catalytic converter, though higher performance variants such as the M340i carry a more complex exhaust system with a greater precious metal loading. On a standard petrol 3 Series from the mid-2000s to early 2010s, the cat can be worth £60 to £120 in isolation. More recent models from 2016 onwards tend to carry cats worth £30 to £70. Diesel 3 Series models use a diesel oxidation catalyst rather than a traditional petrol cat, which carries a lower precious metal value. In all cases, leave the cat on the car when you sell it for scrap to get the full benefit reflected in the dealer's offer.

How to Get the Best Price for Your BMW 3 Series

The 3 Series is a premium car and its scrap value reflects that, but there is still meaningful variation between dealers and it is worth comparing three or more quotes before committing. Because the cat value is higher than average for this car, it is especially important to use a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility that has the equipment to process it properly, as they will factor the full value into their offer. Have the V5C logbook ready and leave all components in place on the day of collection to avoid any deductions.
