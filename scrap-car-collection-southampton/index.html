How to scrap your car in Southampton

Use the comparison form with your registration plate and postcode. You will see live quotes from dealers covering your area. Accept the best offer, confirm the time, and the dealer sends a flatbed truck to collect the car at your address. You hand over the keys and receive your Certificate of Destruction at that point. Payment arrives by bank transfer the same day or the next working day.

Non-runners, cars with no MOT and accident-damaged vehicles are all accepted. See our full guide: How to scrap a car: step by step.

The core factors are your car's kerb weight and the live price of scrap steel.
