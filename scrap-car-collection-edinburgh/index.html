What affects your Edinburgh scrap quote?

The price dealers offer depends mainly on your car's weight and the current price of steel. A larger, heavier car will generally fetch more. The catalytic converter also adds value — most dealers factor it in automatically. Your postcode can affect quotes slightly, as dealers whose collection routes pass through your area can offer more than one making a special trip.

Is it legal to scrap a car in Scotland?

Yes, provided you use a licensed Authorised Treatment Facility. In Scotland, ATFs must be registered with SEPA rather than the Environment Agency (which covers England and Wales), but the process for the vehicle owner is identical. You receive a Certificate of Destruction, and the dealer handles DVLA notification and responsible processing of fluids and materials. Using an unlicensed buyer (often called a "cash buyer" or "man with a van") is not legal and leaves you without a Certificate of Destruction.

Do I need to notify anyone in Scotland?

Yes. After your car has been collected, you should notify the DVLA online at GOV.UK. The ATF is also required to send a notification to the DVLA, but confirming it yourself takes a couple of minutes and ensures your name is removed from the vehicle record. The DVLA will automatically cancel any remaining road tax and refund unused full months. See our guide: Notifying the DVLA when you scrap your car.
